About the Creator & Story

Anchor & Flow was conceived and created by Rishi Roy (The Productivity Guy • LinkedIn Profile).

The Dual Mission Behind Anchor & Flow:
1. Executive Work Velocity: Practicing serial tasking (as used by Napoleon and Elon Musk) to eliminate mundane busywork and channel complete focus into the Top 3 daily priorities.

2. Caring for My Mother’s Glaucoma: This timer was also built out of a deeply personal necessity—to help manage the strict, exact medication intervals for my mother’s glaucoma eye drops. This caregiving journey inspired my newly published book on overcoming glaucoma care routines.

In Anchor & Flow, your immutable meetings and vital health anchors (like eye drops, medications, or meals) are locked with precision, while the Flow Agent packs your high-impact work into serial single-task blocks.

📅 Calendar Export & Help

💡 How It Works: Drop your .ics file right into the chat or click 📎. Anchor & Flow locks meetings as fixed anchors and schedules your focus tasks around them.

🌐 Outlook Web (Office 365)

  1. Go to Outlook on the Web.
  2. Click ⚙️ Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars.
  3. Under Publish a calendar, choose your primary calendar.
  4. Download the ICS file and drop it into the chat!

💻 Outlook Desktop (Windows / Mac)

  1. In the Outlook app, go to Calendar.
  2. Click File → Save Calendar.
  3. Select Today or Tomorrow, save as .ics, and drop it here!

📅 Google Calendar

  1. Open Google Calendar → click ⚙️ Settings.
  2. Click Import & export → Export.
  3. Extract the .ics file from the zip and drop it in!

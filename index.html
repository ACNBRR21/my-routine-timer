1. The Problem with Conventional Timers

Traditional timers create friction because they operate in isolation from your wall-clock commitments. Anchor & Flow bridges this disconnect:

  • Hard Anchors (📅): Meetings imported from Outlook or Google Calendar with a fixed wall-clock start time (e.g., 14:30). They remain pinned to real time.
  • Fluid Flow (⏱): Deep work sprints, review tasks, and ergonomic habits (squats, hydration) that flow dynamically through the open windows between your meetings.

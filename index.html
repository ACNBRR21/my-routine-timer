1. The Core Innovation: Anchors vs. Flow

Traditional timers run in isolation without knowing when you have meetings. Anchor & Flow bridges the gap:

  • Hard Anchors (📅): Meetings imported from Outlook or Google Calendar with a fixed start time (e.g., 14:30). They remain pinned to the real wall-clock.
  • Fluid Flow (⏱): Deep work sprints, review tasks, and ergonomic habits (squats, hydration) that flow dynamically through the open windows between your meetings.

3. Key Controls

  • Finish Early (⏩): Finished ahead of time? Tap to log exact wall-clock seconds saved and instantly advance.
  • Time Extensions (+1m to +1h): Tap anytime—even when the alarm sounds—to add time, silence the alert, and continue working without penalty.
  • Zen Mode (⊙): Press Z on your keyboard to eliminate all distractions and display only the countdown and your next meeting anchor.

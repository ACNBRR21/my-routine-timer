🔗 How to Share & Link Your Calendar URLs

Anchor & Flow connects directly to your calendar share links (iCal / Webcal / ICS). No manual file uploading needed!

Zero-File Upload
⚡ Why Share Links instead of File Uploads?
File uploads are static and become outdated within hours. By linking your Google or Outlook iCal share URL, Anchor & Flow pulls your live schedule and locks your meetings as immutable Anchor Events without you ever having to re-upload files.

📅 Google Calendar — Getting Your Secret iCal URL

  1. Open Google Calendar on your desktop browser.
  2. On the left panel, find "My calendars", hover over your primary calendar, and click the three dots (•••) → Settings and sharing.
  3. Scroll down to the "Integrate calendar" section.
  4. Find the box labeled "Secret address in iCal format" (or public address if your calendar is public).
  5. Click the copy button to copy the https://calendar.google.com/calendar/ical/.../basic.ics URL.
  6. In Anchor & Flow, go to Overview & Profile → click + Add Calendar Link → paste the URL and click Link This Calendar!

💻 Microsoft Outlook on the Web (Office 365 / Outlook.com)

  1. Open Outlook on the Web.
  2. Click the ⚙️ Settings (gear) icon in the top-right corner.
  3. Navigate to Calendar → Shared calendars.
  4. Under "Publish a calendar", select your primary calendar and choose "Can view all details".
  5. Click Publish. Outlook will display an HTML link and an ICS link.
  6. Click and copy the ICS link (starts with https://outlook.office.com/owa/calendar/.../reachcalendar.ics).
  7. Paste this link into Overview & Profile in Anchor & Flow!

🍏 Apple iCloud Calendar (Webcal URL)

  1. Open the Calendar app on your Mac (or visit iCloud.com).
  2. Hover over the calendar in the sidebar and click the share icon (or right-click → Share Calendar).
  3. Check the box for Public Calendar.
  4. Copy the generated Webcal share URL (starts with webcal://p...-caldav.icloud.com/...).
  5. Paste into Anchor & Flow → your events will sync automatically!

⚡ Intelligent Multi-Calendar Deduplication

How Anchor & Flow solves the "double meeting" headache when you link both work and personal calendars.

Many professionals have their work meetings forwarded to their personal calendar, or are invited to the same client call on both Outlook and Google Calendar. In traditional apps, this creates ugly duplicates, showing the exact same meeting twice side-by-side.

How Anchor & Flow Deduplicates:

  • Signature Matching: Our local engine analyzes the meeting title and start time across all linked calendars.
  • Intelligent Merging: If an "Executive Sprint Sync" at 10:00 AM appears on both your Work Outlook and Personal Google calendars, Anchor & Flow unifies them into one clean card.
  • Clear Attribution: The meeting card displays a badge: ⚡ Unified across Work Outlook & Personal Google, keeping your dashboard pristine.

🎯 Harvard Timeboxing & Serial Tasking

The science of high-leverage execution used by Napoleon, Elon Musk, and top performers.

1. The Harvard Rule of 3

Identify no more than three high-leverage tasks for the day (Top 1, Top 2, Top 3). Once these three are done, your day is a definitive victory.

2. Serial Single-Tasking

Multitasking degrades cognitive performance by up to 40%. Serial tasking demands 100% focus on one milestone until the timer chimes, followed by an intentional calming pause.

3. Anchor vs. Flow Blocks

Meetings and essential health rituals are immutable Anchors. Deep work, writing, and review blocks are flexible Flows packed tightly around them.

⏱️ Timer+ & Execution Rhythms: Complete Manual

Modeled after the acclaimed Apple iOS Timer+ app—versatile timing from 1-tap focus countdowns to complex multi-step interval routines.

Timer+ Engine
Anchor & Flow's execution engine is built upon the core philosophy of Timer+: delivering ultra-clean, Apple-inspired timing with zero cognitive friction. Whether you are running a single deep work block, managing glaucoma care drops, or pushing through a high-intensity workout, the timing suite handles it seamlessly.

⏳ 1. Countdown Timers

Designed for focused execution, study sessions, reading, and non-negotiable health routines:

  • Flexible Durations: Set any duration in Hours, Minutes, and Seconds (or up to 99 days for extended tracking).
  • Optional Repeats: Configure countdowns to repeat from 1 up to 64 consecutive times with subtle harmonic bell chimes between cycles.
  • On-The-Fly Time Adjustments: Add or subtract time (-1m, +1m, +5m) directly from the active execution player without pausing or resetting your timer.
  • Overtime Count-Up: Once a countdown completes, the timer gently chimes and counts upward so you know exactly how long you have rested or exceeded your block.

⏲️ 2. Stopwatches & Lap Recording

For open-ended focus sprints, writing flows, meditations, therapy, and speeches:

  • Count-Up Tracking: Starts cleanly from 00:00.00 with smooth millisecond accuracy.
  • Named Lap Recording: Tap + Lap during execution to record split times (e.g., Lap 1: 05:22, Lap 2: 12:40) visible in real-time.
  • Pause, Resume & Reset: Full state preservation across browser sessions.

📅 3. Target Date & Time Countdowns

Track critical milestones, medical appointments, deadlines, and book launches:

  • Specific Date/Time Target: Select any future date and clock time using the native date-time picker.
  • Live Day & Time Ticker: Displays days, hours, minutes, and seconds remaining (e.g. 2d 04:12:30).
  • Post-Deadline Count-Up: Once the deadline passes, indicates overtime with a clear + badge.

🔄 4. Custom & Interval Routines (Multi-Segment Builder)

Build complex, multi-segment sequences with customized intervals, rounds, and restorative rest:

  • Multi-Segment Construction: Add as many steps as needed (e.g., Warmup 5m → High-Intensity Work 20s → Rest Interval 10s → Cooldown 5m).
  • Rounds & Sets: Multiply routines across 1 to 10 rounds with seamless cycle loops.
  • Audio Announcements & Chimes: Harmonic synthesizer announces transitions so you can execute eyes-free.
  • Skip or Restart Steps: Tap ⏭ to advance to the next segment or ↺ to restart the active step.

🧴 5. Glaucoma Medication Protocol (Rishi Roy's Book Origin)

Rigid, infallible timing is critical for glaucoma caregiving. Premature or delayed eye drops compromise intraocular pressure stability:

  • Primary Medication Interval (1:30:00+): Mandatory recovery pause between primary pressure-reducing prostaglandins and secondary carbonic anhydrase inhibitors.
  • Secondary Hydration Dose (1:15:00): Regulated timing for artificial tear hydration to avoid washing out active therapeutic compounds.
  • Book Reference: These care routines reflect the clinical discipline detailed in Rishi Roy's book on glaucoma bio-rhythms and caregiving methodology.

📁 Custom Folders & Library Organization:

Keep your timers and routines organized by project, client, workout, or caregiving rhythm:

  • Filter View: Tap any folder pill at the top of the timers screen (e.g. Work & Focus, Health & Care, Workouts, Daily Routines) to view relevant timers.
  • Create New Folders: Tap ⚙️ Manage Folders on the folder bar or open the header menu (•••) → enter a custom folder name (e.g. Client Projects, Study) → tap + Add Folder.
  • Rename & Delete: In the Manage Folders modal, rename any custom folder or tap 🗑️ to remove it. Timers in deleted folders are safely preserved and moved to Work & Focus.
  • AI Planner Integration: Harvard Timebox plans generated from the AI Day Planner are stored into Daily Routines so you can run them today, tomorrow, or anytime, and delete them when finished.

🚀 Quick Steps to Create Any Timer:

  1. Open Timers & Routines and tap the floating + button at the bottom-right.
  2. Choose your type: Timer, Stopwatch, Countdown, or Routine.
  3. Configure title, folder category, duration, and optional notes.
  4. Tap Save & Add to store it in your library, or start it instantly with 1 tap.

🔒 Privacy Policy & Data Sovereignty

Anchor & Flow is engineered from the ground up on a local-first, zero-telemetry architecture.

100% Local Storage
We believe personal productivity, health schedules, and executive agendas are deeply private. Unlike conventional cloud software that harvests user calendars and behavioral analytics, Anchor & Flow enforces complete client-side data sovereignty.

💾 1. 100% Client-Side Storage

All your profile information, focus durations, custom timers, routine steps, and meeting agendas are stored exclusively in your browser's private local storage. No user accounts, passwords, or tracking cookies are required.

🌐 2. Ephemeral Calendar Sync

When you synchronize a Google, Outlook, or Apple calendar share URL, the feed is retrieved via direct HTTPS or encrypted stateless CORS relays solely to parse meeting timestamps in browser memory. Your URLs are never recorded on external servers.

🧴 3. Health & Caregiving Confidentiality

Medication intervals (including glaucoma eye drops and personal wellness anchors) are processed purely in offline client execution. No biometric, medical, or diagnostic telemetry is ever transmitted.

📤 4. Instant Export & Total Wipe

You maintain total data ownership. From Overview & Profile, you can download a human-readable user_preferences.txt, export a full JSON backup, or permanently purge all data with a single tap.

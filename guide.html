🔗 How to Share & Link Your Calendar URLs

Anchor & Flow connects directly to your calendar share links (iCal / Webcal / ICS). No manual file uploading needed!

Zero-File Upload
⚡ Why Share Links instead of File Uploads?
File uploads are static and become outdated within hours. By linking your Google or Outlook iCal share URL, Anchor & Flow pulls your live schedule and locks your meetings as immutable Anchor Events without you ever having to re-upload files.

📅 Google Calendar — Getting Your Secret iCal URL

  1. Open Google Calendar on your desktop browser.
  2. On the left panel, find "My calendars", hover over your primary calendar, and click the three dots (•••) → Settings and sharing.
  3. Scroll down to the "Integrate calendar" section.
  4. Find the box labeled "Secret address in iCal format" (or public address if your calendar is public).
  5. Click the copy button to copy the https://calendar.google.com/calendar/ical/.../basic.ics URL.
  6. In Anchor & Flow, go to Overview & Profile → click + Add Calendar Link → paste the URL and click Link This Calendar!

💻 Microsoft Outlook on the Web (Office 365 / Outlook.com)

  1. Open Outlook on the Web.
  2. Click the ⚙️ Settings (gear) icon in the top-right corner.
  3. Navigate to Calendar → Shared calendars.
  4. Under "Publish a calendar", select your primary calendar and choose "Can view all details".
  5. Click Publish. Outlook will display an HTML link and an ICS link.
  6. Click and copy the ICS link (starts with https://outlook.office.com/owa/calendar/.../reachcalendar.ics).
  7. Paste this link into Overview & Profile in Anchor & Flow!

🍏 Apple iCloud Calendar (Webcal URL)

  1. Open the Calendar app on your Mac (or visit iCloud.com).
  2. Hover over the calendar in the sidebar and click the share icon (or right-click → Share Calendar).
  3. Check the box for Public Calendar.
  4. Copy the generated Webcal share URL (starts with webcal://p...-caldav.icloud.com/...).
  5. Paste into Anchor & Flow → your events will sync automatically!

⚡ Intelligent Multi-Calendar Deduplication

How Anchor & Flow solves the "double meeting" headache when you link both work and personal calendars.

Many professionals have their work meetings forwarded to their personal calendar, or are invited to the same client call on both Outlook and Google Calendar. In traditional apps, this creates ugly duplicates, showing the exact same meeting twice side-by-side.

How Anchor & Flow Deduplicates:

  • Signature Matching: Our local engine analyzes the meeting title and start time across all linked calendars.
  • Intelligent Merging: If an "Executive Sprint Sync" at 10:00 AM appears on both your Work Outlook and Personal Google calendars, Anchor & Flow unifies them into one clean card.
  • Clear Attribution: The meeting card displays a badge: ⚡ Unified across Work Outlook & Personal Google, keeping your dashboard pristine.

🎯 Harvard Timeboxing & Serial Tasking

The science of high-leverage execution used by Napoleon, Elon Musk, and top performers.

1. The Harvard Rule of 3

Identify no more than three high-leverage tasks for the day (Top 1, Top 2, Top 3). Once these three are done, your day is a definitive victory.

2. Serial Single-Tasking

Multitasking degrades cognitive performance by up to 40%. Serial tasking demands 100% focus on one milestone until the timer chimes, followed by an intentional calming pause.

3. Anchor vs. Flow Blocks

Meetings and essential health rituals are immutable Anchors. Deep work, writing, and review blocks are flexible Flows packed tightly around them.

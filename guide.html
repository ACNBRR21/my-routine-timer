The Anchor & Flow Method

A First-Principles Guide to Dynamic Scheduling, Outlook Ingestion, and Execution Auditing

1. Philosophy: Anchors vs. Flow

Traditional timers fail because they run in isolation from real-world commitments. Anchor & Flow divides tasks into two classes:

  • Hard Anchors (📅): Immovable calendar commitments (meetings, syncs, webinars).
  • Fluid Flow (⏱): Focused tasks, deep work blocks, and physical resets that dynamically flow through the open windows between your meetings.

2. Generating Schedules via AI Prompting

Paste your day's calendar agenda and raw to-do list into an LLM using the prompt:

Routine,Task Name,Duration (Mins),Parent Task,Scheduled Start
Work Day,Deep Work: Strategic Roadmap,50,,
Work Day,Hydrate & 10 Squats,2,,
Work Day,Executive Sync (Outlook),45,,14:30

Leave Scheduled Start empty for flexible tasks. Populate with HH:MM only for hard calendar meetings.

3. The Estimation Multiplier

At the end of your day, export your audit CSV. Calculate your personal multiplier:

=Actual_Elapsed / Target_Duration

Calibrating your future estimates against this ratio closes the gap between planned and actual task completion.
